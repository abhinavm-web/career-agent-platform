# H-1B configuration and company research

Open **H-1B research** in the local dashboard, or select **Research H-1B records and jobs** on an opportunity. Set a new-sponsorship/transfer preference, earliest fiscal year for history counts, and whether to show jobs whose posting policy is unknown. These preferences are local search settings, not work-authorization answers or an eligibility determination.

The opportunity browser works immediately with saved job descriptions, without imported government records. Filter by role, employer, location, and approximate posting signal; search results are paginated and respect the saved new-sponsorship/transfer preference. Use **Find more jobs with JobSpy** to open discovery. Explicit positive, restrictive, unclear and absent wording are signals from the saved description, not verified employer commitments; check the original posting and confirm the specific H-1B path. The browser does not fetch jobs by itself.

On the main Opportunity board, **Imported employer history** finds loaded jobs whose exact displayed employer name, or explicitly saved job-specific employer association, has any imported DOL LCA or USCIS decision records. A separate badge and detail identify the sources and matched name; they do not change the posting policy, score, packet or approval. The indicator covers all imported years and statuses, including denials, and does not establish legal identity, current sponsorship or eligibility. Reusable company suggestions are not applied automatically. Refresh the board after imports or association changes, then open H-1B research to inspect the records, dates and source. No badge means no matching local records were found, not that the employer lacks history.

Search uses a company-name substring against imported legal employer names and companies in stored jobs. Employer results are paginated in groups of 25, including the name lookup inside each job's association form. Choosing a name fills the form; a source note and explicit confirmation are still required to save it. Job results can include matching stored opportunities beyond the board's browser loading limits. Changing the query clears earlier results. No automatic external search runs. Use the Opportunity board's explicit job discovery to add postings separately. Broader punctuation-insensitive searches group exact imported names first, but still scan local filing data and can take time on large imports; similar names remain separate candidates.

In company research and a job's employer-association form, the optional broader name search ignores punctuation and spaces to help find spelling variants. Company research applies this option to both imported filing names and saved job company names. It is a substring search, not entity resolution: unrelated companies can appear, and brands or subsidiaries may still be missed. Filing histories remain separate under their exact case/whitespace-normalized names; choosing a result does not link or merge records until you review and explicitly save the job association. Large local datasets can make this broader scan slower.

## Load public records

The **Data administration** panel shows the stored DOL coverage and recent imports alongside USCIS snapshots. Choose **Check DOL releases** and confirm the disclosure to request the fixed official performance page; this sends no profile or job data. The app identifies the latest supported quarterly workbook link it recognizes on that page. This is a page observation, not a guarantee that the release is complete, the workbook can be downloaded, or the resulting records are current. The check does not download the workbook or change stored data. Use **Use this workbook URL** to copy the identified link into the separate download form, then review and explicitly confirm that action. If the page is blocked, unavailable, or uses unsupported links, the check reports an error and leaves prior records intact; inspect the official page and use a local file if needed. The panel is a local control, not an authenticated administrator role.

For supported quarterly releases, paste the direct `https://www.dol.gov/media/LCA_Disclosure_Data_FYyyyy_Qn.xlsx` URL from the official performance page, or use a link from the explicit release check, and explicitly select **Download and import DOL workbook**. The app connects only to public DOL HTTPS addresses, verifies TLS, follows no redirects, sends no profile data or credentials, and limits the download to 512 MiB and 20 minutes. It then validates and stages the workbook locally before one atomic apply. Failures leave existing records intact. The import ledger records the fetched file's SHA-256, byte count, source URL and capture date as `downloaded_from_dol_https`; HTTPS transport and the fingerprint do not establish that the release is complete, current, or substantively accurate. If DOL blocks the request or uses a different release path, download the official file yourself and use the local import below. Check release dates and coverage on DOL's site; no automatic source checks or imports run.

1. Visit the official [DOL OFLC performance data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) page and download an LCA disclosure workbook. In the H-1B research import panel, provide the source URL and capture date, choose the original XLSX (up to 512 MB), and confirm the provenance. Install workbook support first (`pip install -e '.[lca]'`; the Docker image includes it). The upload is streamed to a temporary local file, removed after processing, and runs the same atomic validation as the CLI. Large files may take time and substantial temporary disk space. The panel reports upload, structure-scan and row-reading progress; those counts are not committed records. Wait for the final success message. The app does not independently verify a supplied file or its source.

   A second tab or a reloaded page can observe an active workbook upload or download after opening H-1B research. Once it stops, that observer cannot know whether it succeeded: refresh coverage and inspect recent import history before retrying. Progress is in memory and is lost if the server restarts; the observer does not track CSV imports or imports run from the CLI.

   Alternatively, use the command below to import the original XLSX without a CSV conversion:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pip install -e '.[lca]'
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m career_agent.cli --db .local/demo.sqlite3 h1b-import .local/lca.xlsx --source-url "https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance" --captured-on YYYY-MM-DD
```

Use the date you downloaded the file, not an assumed reporting cutoff. The file is parsed locally in read-only mode. Selected public fields are validated and staged in temporary storage before the workspace transaction begins; the final apply rechecks against current records and either commits the entire import or rolls it back. Unrelated edits can proceed during staging, though the final apply temporarily serializes database writes. Temporary storage requires additional free disk space. It remains labeled user-imported and not independently verified. The workbook must have exactly one sheet with the required LCA headers and be no larger than 512 MB; large workbooks can take time and memory, and Excel-compressed data is bounded. Worksite and appendix sheets do not substitute for the main disclosure data. Direct government download is not performed by this command.

If you prefer CSV:
2. Export the disclosure sheet as **UTF-8 CSV**, preserving its DOL column headers. Do not use the worksite/appendix sheet as the main disclosure sheet.
3. In the import panel, supply the original DOL source URL and the date you captured that source, select the CSV and confirm provenance. The browser accepts files up to 8 MB (the encoded API request is limited to 10 MB). Larger CSVs can be streamed through the CLI:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m career_agent.cli --db .local/demo.sqlite3 h1b-import .local/lca.csv --source-url "https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance" --captured-on YYYY-MM-DD
```

Replace the date with the actual capture date and preferably cite the specific disclosure workbook URL. All storage remains in the selected local SQLite database. Locally supplied imports are labeled **user_imported_not_independently_verified**: an official-domain URL supplied by you is not proof that the file was fetched or verified. Government requests occur only when you explicitly choose a supported refresh; the app does not scrape third-party sponsorship sites. No API credentials are needed.

Required columns are `CASE_NUMBER`, `EMPLOYER_NAME`, `VISA_CLASS`, `CASE_STATUS`, `DECISION_DATE`. Optional displayed columns are `EMPLOYER_CITY`, `EMPLOYER_STATE`, `JOB_TITLE`. Other columns, including FEINs, attorney details and personal contacts, are not stored. ISO dates, ISO dates with midnight time, `MM/DD/YYYY`, and Excel date cells are supported. USCIS employer CSVs are not supported by this importer.

## Evidence and edge cases

- The [DOL record layout](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/oflc/pdfs/LCA_Record_Layout_FY2025_Q3.pdf) describes the supported fields. Only `H-1B` rows enter this index. H-1B1 Chile/Singapore and E-3 Australian rows are counted separately as skipped; unrecognized visa classes reject the import.
- Certified, certified-withdrawn, denied and withdrawn cases have separate counts. Only currently stored **Certified** cases contribute to the recent-certified count. These counts represent distinct LCA case numbers, not workers, petitions, approvals or vacancies.
- Fiscal year is derived from decision date with the October 1 boundary. Source capture dates cannot be in the future or precede the decision. A configurable year window limits counts; the latest 20 records remain available for context even outside that window.
- Case numbers deduplicate overlapping releases. A later stored decision or capture date blocks an older replacement. Same-case, same-capture-date conflicting content rejects the entire import. Invalid rows roll back all writes from that import; no partial success is concealed. Updating a case retains its latest imported state, not an immutable archive of earlier source versions.
- The research coverage panel lists the ten most recent successful DOL imports, with the supplied URL and capture date, import time, observed range of H-1B decision dates, row outcome counts and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the selected fields in row order. The fingerprint is not a checksum of the original file; fields that are not imported are excluded. The observed range is not a verified reporting period, and the ledger does not establish file authenticity or completeness. Failed and empty imports produce no ledger entry. Imports made before this feature have no ledger entry and remain unverified.
- Search suggestions are not entity matches. Automatic job-history joins use case/whitespace-normalized exact employer names only. Punctuation, suffix differences, brands, subsidiaries, acquisitions and staffing clients are not silently merged. Even an exact name can be ambiguous; inspect the legal entity, city/state and original source. For a saved job, you may associate an exact name already in the imported records, recording how you checked it and acknowledging that it is unverified. The association applies only to that job, can be removed, and retains a local revision history. It does not establish entity identity, current hiring, sponsorship or eligibility; the posting assessment remains independent.
- A candidate may also save a reusable research suggestion for a displayed company name, with a note and revision history. It is shared only by case/whitespace-normalized exact displayed names and is never applied to another job automatically. Similar names may refer to distinct entities or staffing clients, so each job requires its own confirmed association. Removing the reusable suggestion retains its history and does not change existing job associations.
- Coverage is always labeled incomplete. Empty results, old fiscal years, an empty dataset or missing matches mean **unknown**, never proof of no sponsorship. Source dates are visible; recent capture does not imply a current role-level commitment.
- Employer and job cards distinguish no DOL data imported, no exact-name match, matches only before the chosen fiscal-year window, and records in that window. USCIS separately distinguishes no snapshot, no snapshot in the window, no exact-name match in the window, and returned records. The counts remain descriptions of the local dataset, not proof of employer identity, complete coverage, or sponsorship for a role.
- Posting rules preserve matching text and distinguish `excluded`, `conflicting`, `conditional`, `transfer_only`, `transfer_supported`, `possible` and `unknown`. Explicit exclusions override history. Conflicting support/restrictions require clarification. Conditional language and screening questions do not become positive claims. Generic sponsorship wording requires H-1B category confirmation. Transfer support alone leaves new sponsorship unspecified; only explicit exclusivity is called transfer-only. Explicit new-sponsorship support is preserved when transfers are also supported. Unrelated restrictions such as remote-work availability do not negate sponsorship.
- Posting interpretation is a deterministic English-language heuristic, not complete natural-language understanding. All stored job links require a fresh check: this feature does not verify that the vacancy remains open, that sponsorship applies to the location, or that an individual qualifies for transfer, cap exemption or a new petition. No legal screening answers are inferred.
- Recognized restrictions include explicit requirements to work without employer sponsorship and statements that employment-based visa sponsorship is not offered or available. The original matching text remains visible for review; other phrasing or context may be missed or misinterpreted.
- A restriction explicitly limited to other or unspecified roles is marked for clarification rather than applied to this role, unless the text also explicitly restricts the current role. This is a limited wording rule, not a complete determination of scope.
- A recognized sponsorship restriction followed by an adjacent “unless” exception is marked conditional when no independent restriction is detected. This limited rule does not interpret the exception or determine whether a candidate meets it; other exception wording may be missed.
- Statements limiting sponsorship to a group or condition, or saying it cannot be guaranteed, are treated as conditional rather than general support. Explicitly denied H-1B transfers alongside a separate positive sponsorship claim are shown as conflicting. Other wording can still be missed; inspect the original posting.

For a saved job, expand **Recruiter or employer policy notes** to record the date, source, pathway, reported response and what was said, including conditions. These are candidate-entered research notes, not independently verified confirmations or legal advice. They apply only to that job and never change the automated posting assessment, filing history, matching, packets or approvals. Reload the section to see the latest 50 notes in recording order; add a new note to correct an earlier one. Notes are append-only in this version and can contain personal information; like the local database, they are unencrypted and included in full workspace backups.

The existing packet evidence files remain a separate, manually curated input. Importing LCA records does not modify saved packets, approvals or candidate facts. [USCIS refresh/import and live ATS checks](USCIS_LIVE_CHECKS.md) are available as separate, explicit actions. Checks for other job sites remain future work.

## Validation

Unit/API tests cover entity boundaries, case deduplication, atomic rollback, stale decisions, unsupported visa/status rows, fiscal-year boundaries, authentication, source dates, literal searches, pagination and posting-language edge cases. The optional real-browser test imports a fictional CSV, saves preferences, searches, checks transfer-only behavior and verifies that untrusted record text cannot execute HTML. Enable it with `CAREER_AGENT_TEST_BROWSER=1` after installing Playwright Chromium. CI runs it alongside the existing suite.
